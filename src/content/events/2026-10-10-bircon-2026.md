BIRCON is a pen & paper, tabletop roleplaying game convention run by fans for fans.

It is organized by a group of roleplaying games enthusiasts from Berlin as part of BIRCON UG. See Contact and Impressum for more information.

BIRCON is now in its third year, and our continued growth shows our commitment to making it the convention for international RPGs in Berlin.
