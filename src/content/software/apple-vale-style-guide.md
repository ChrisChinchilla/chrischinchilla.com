[Apple Style Guide](https://github.com/ChrisChinchilla/Apple-style-guide) is a [Vale](https://vale.sh) linter style implementing rules from the [Apple Style Guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/applestyleguide/) (June 2025 edition), so teams following Apple's writing conventions can lint for them automatically.

It's distributed as a downloadable release: copy the `apple` directory into your Vale `StylesPath` and add it to `BasedOnStyles` in your `.vale.ini`. It's still a work in progress, welcomes contributions, and — as the repo makes clear — isn't maintained or endorsed by Apple.
