The Trello extension for [Raycast](https://www.raycast.com/) brings Trello into Raycast's launcher: fetching and searching cards assigned to you, searching and browsing boards, viewing a board's lists and members, creating cards, and moving or deleting cards, all without opening Trello itself.

It's part of the community-maintained [raycast/extensions](https://github.com/raycast/extensions) repository. I'm a contributor to this extension.
